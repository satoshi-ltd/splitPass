Browser extension

The browser extension: popup, in-page panel and camera-access page, rendered with the real stylesheets from browser-extension/ and the markup its scripts build. The popup is 360 px wide, exactly as Chrome and Brave show it.

Theme

The extension picks light or dark only from the operating system (prefers-color-scheme in theme.css); it has no setting of its own. This page mirrors the switch above by re-declaring the same variables under :root[data-theme] in the generated extension-tokens.css, read from theme.css, so the real stylesheets can be shown in both modes. The camera scrim, --color-on-inverse and --color-overlay-soft stay light in both, as in the extension.

Vault

First run asks for a master password; afterwards the popup opens on the unlock form. Everything else is hidden until the vault is unlocked.

Scanner

The camera starts by itself when the vault is unlocked and nothing is saved for the site. Live video cannot be drawn here, so the stage shows its resting background.

Saved for this site

Passwords scanned on a domain are kept encrypted for it. Each row shows when it expires; click the expiry text to cycle Auto, 1 year and No expiry.

In-page panel

A fixed 360 px panel in the top-right corner of the page, inside a closed shadow root, opened by focusing a password field with an unlocked vault. It never uses the camera.

Camera access page

A normal browser tab, opened by Fix camera permission so the address bar can unblock a denied camera. It closes itself once access is granted.
