Design system

split/Pass keeps secrets you can split between people and places. The interface is square, flat and dotted: one typeface, one orange, no shadows. Every specimen is drawn from the values that ship, in the app and in the browser extension.

Rules 10

Derived from the code; each one is checkable in the sources named on the pages.

  • Square and flat: every radius is 0 and nothing casts a shadow, in the app (radius = 0) and in the extension (--radius-* : 0, --shadow-* : none). radiusFull is defined and unused.
  • One typeface, three weights: Doto Medium for text, Bold for labels and buttons, Black for titles, the wordmark and emphasis.
  • Accent orange #F46A3A is the only brand colour. Titles, primary buttons, the favourite tile, the active tab and the scanner footer use it; text on it is onAccent.
  • Spacing comes from seven steps and screens keep a 24 px side inset (viewOffset). Inputs are 48 px, buttons 36 / 44 / 52 px, tap targets at least 44 px.
  • Inputs sit on surface with a 1 px border; focus turns the border to accent. Secrets are masked with * until the eye is pressed.
  • Secrets are shown on a footer strip: black in the light theme, the light QR ground in the dark theme, accent in the scanner. Digits in a revealed value take the accent colour.
  • The scanner is always dark, whatever the theme, and the NFC card is always drawn with the light palette.
  • Feedback is a banner that slides in from the top for 3 seconds (errors stay); confirmation is a bottom sheet with Cancel and Accept.
  • Motion is short: 220 ms for quick transitions, 320 ms for standard ones.
  • The extension follows the OS for light and dark, is 360 px wide as a popup and as an in-page panel, and hides everything but the unlock form until the vault is unlocked.
