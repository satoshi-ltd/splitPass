Onboarding

Four slides, then the master passphrase setup. Shown while the vault is not configured or onboarding has not finished.

Master passphrase

One screen, four modes: setup, sign-in, backup import and backup export. Failures come back as notifications and, for sign-in, as a warning card.

Home

The vault list. Favorites first, then letter sections; the summary counts strong against mediocre secrets.

Create

Password, card and seed phrase share one form; the fields adapt to what is typed. 2FA swaps the secret for a QR scanner.

Viewer

The secret as a QR on top and its text below. The footer is black in light mode and light in dark mode; digits take the accent colour once revealed.

Scanner

QR and NFC share one screen; the accent footer appears as soon as something was read. The header is always black.

Settings

Grouped rows: toggles, navigation and values. Reset Data is the only danger row.

Password generator

Opened from Create as a picker. Copy also fills the form and goes back. The strength label follows the configuration.

Dialogs

The confirm route is a transparent modal over the previous screen with a bottom sheet.

Notifications

A banner at the top: dark for information, accent for results of a scan or a card, dark with a red icon for errors. Tap to dismiss; errors stay until dismissed.

Dark

The same screens with the dark palette, forced on regardless of the page theme.
