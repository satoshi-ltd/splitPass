UI-SCANNER-PERMISSION · Scanner · light theme · task

A blocked camera says the same sentence twice and hides its button

When the camera is blocked, Scanner.qr.js prints "Camera access is blocked. Enable it in system settings to scan QR secrets." as the title and again as the caption, and the outlined button has no tone, so its label takes the content colour: dark text on the 0.85 black scrim in the light theme.

UI-COPY-PLURALS · Home · Settings · NFC card · task

One secret is plural, and the card has two spellings

UNLOCK-FORMAT-WIPE · Unlock · decision · task

An unreadable vault is erased before anyone can offer a backup

On ERR_PERSISTENCE_FORMAT the unlock screen erases all local data, resets to onboarding and shows a notification; the third wrong passphrase does the same. The creator decides whether the format case keeps wiping at once or first offers a backup import; the copy on the right is illustrative, and the three-wrong-passphrases wipe is recorded in SPEC either way.
